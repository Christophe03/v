## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, vibrant, and dynamic visual language tailored for an artisanal food and local produce marketplace. The aesthetic balances contemporary cleanliness with earthy, appetizing energy.

### Brand Personality
- **Warm & Inviting:** Cultivates a welcoming atmosphere that mirrors a local farmer's market.
- **Dynamic & Fresh:** Celebrates the vitality of natural, high-quality ingredients through lively color accents.
- **Trustworthy & Grounded:** Professional structure ensures clear navigation and reliable e-commerce functionality.

### Design Style
A blend of **Modern Minimalism** and **Tactile Warmth**. Clean layouts, generous whitespace, and high-legibility typography provide a contemporary digital storefront, while rich natural colors and organic forms deliver sensory appeal.

## Colors

The color palette captures the essence of fresh produce: lush greens, sun-ripened yellows, and rich harvest reds, anchored by clean, crisp neutrals to let product photography shine.

- **Primary (Fresh Green):** Conveys organic quality, sustainability, and natural health.
- **Secondary (Harvest Yellow):** Evokes warmth, sunshine, and agricultural abundance.
- **Tertiary (Ripe Red):** Draws the eye to key actions, promotional badges, and category highlights.
- **Neutrals:** Soft off-whites and charcoal grays provide a high-contrast, readable backdrop for vibrant food imagery.

## Typography

Typography pairs a warm, approachable geometric sans-serif for headings with a highly legible, neutral sans-serif for interface text and product details.

- **Headlines:** Set in geometric weights to create friendly, modern emphasis.
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif optimized for scanning ingredient lists, origin stories, and pricing.
- **Scale:** Fluid scaling ensures large promotional headers adapt gracefully to mobile viewports without sacrificing impact.

## Layout & Spacing

A **fluid grid system** is employed to ensure product listings and editorial content adapt seamlessly from mobile devices to widescreen desktop displays.

- **Grid Structure:** 12-column fluid grid on desktop, scaling down to 4 columns on mobile.
- **Rhythm:** Generous margins and consistent component spacing provide a breathable, unhurried browsing experience that emphasizes the artisanal nature of the goods.
- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (< 768px), Tablet (768px - 1024px), Desktop (> 1024px).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through **tonal layers and ambient shadows** rather than harsh outlines, creating a soft, tactile interface that feels approachable.

- **Surface Tiers:** Backgrounds use off-white surfaces layered over crisp white cards to group related product details.
- **Shadow Character:** Low-opacity, diffused shadows tinted with subtle green undertones give interactive elements (like product cards and sticky add-to-cart bars) a gentle lift off the page.

## Shapes

A **rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2`, 0.5rem base radius, up to 1.5rem for large containers) softens the UI, banishing sharp corners to reinforce a friendly, organic, and accessible brand feel. 

- **Buttons & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped or generously rounded to invite touch interaction.
- **Cards & Containers:** Softly rounded corners frame photography and pricing clearly without feeling corporate or rigid.

## Components

Consistent styling guidelines for core e-commerce elements:

- **Buttons:** Primary actions utilize solid brand green with high-contrast text and generous padding. Secondary actions use subtle ghost or tonal fills. Destructive or urgent promotional actions leverage harvest red.
- **Chips & Tags:** Pill-shaped metadata indicators used for dietary preferences (e.g., *Bio*, *Local*, *Sans Gluten*), utilizing soft pastel variants of the primary and secondary colors.
- **Lists:** Clean vertical spacing with generous touch targets for cart items, filter menus, and navigation links.
- **Checkboxes & Radio Filters:** Custom-styled with primary green accents for selecting delivery slots, quantities, or filter facets.
- **Input Fields:** Generously padded text fields with clear focus states for search bars, checkout forms, and quantity selectors.
- **Cards:** Elevated product cards featuring edge-to-edge imagery, clear typography hierarchy for pricing, quick "Add to Cart" triggers, and subtle hover lifts.
- **Additional Components:** Includes quantity steppers, sticky mobile checkout bars, category carousels with snap-scroll, and trust badges for local sourcing.
